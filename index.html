The problem

Spreadsheets weren't built to run a distribution business

Manual sheets get out of sync, sales reps see numbers they shouldn't, and owners find out about a bad month after it's over. PharmaTracker replaces that with one live, permissioned system.

Scattered, stale data

Sales, stock, and customer records living in five different files, never quite in sync — and always a version behind.

No access control

Everyone sees everything, or nobody sees anything. There's no middle ground for managers, analysts, and viewers.

Slow, manual reporting

Building a monthly report means hours of copy-pasting instead of minutes of reading a dashboard that's already built.

Who it's for

Sales managers & analysts

Field reps & viewers

Why PharmaTracker

Purpose-built for pharma distribution, not bent to fit it

  • Designed around how distributors actually sell — by product, by customer, by rep — not a generic CRM template.
  • Local payment rails (JazzCash, EasyPaisa) and an admin-approval flow suited to how teams here actually manage access.
  • Fast by default — the whole dashboard runs client-side, so it stays quick even on modest connections.
  • Built to grow with you — from a one-person operation to a multi-role sales team, without switching tools.

Our vision

We're building toward a future where every distributor — not just the largest ones — has real-time visibility into their business. That means deeper forecasting, automated reorder alerts, and multi-branch support are next on the roadmap, all without losing the speed and simplicity you get today.

Booker & Product Target Assignment
Enter a Total Target, then pick a Booker and a percentage (1%–100%) — the total target is divided automatically. No individual product targets needed.

The Booker's share of the Total Target above is calculated automatically from the percentage above and split evenly across the Booker's products — see the Booker / Product Breakdown table above, in Unit-wise or Value-wise view.

Enter a product's Total Unit Target above (after selecting it from the Product dropdown), then assign Booker % as usual — each Booker's Unit Target is that product's unit target multiplied by their percentage (e.g. 1,000 units at 25% = 250 units). Switch the View dropdown to Unit-wise to see Unit Target, Sale, Achievement % and Remaining in units.
